\caption{The lesion segmentation decoder. Structurally this head is identical to the kidney decoder and holds the same 1,572,833 parameters; what differs is its supervision and its downstream use. Its logits are supervised by a soft Dice loss against a union of rasterised lesion bounding boxes, and additionally by the anatomical consistency term, which penalises predicted lesion probability mass falling outside the ground-truth kidney region. Beyond the loss, the same logits are passed through a sigmoid and \emph{detached} to form the spatial attention map used by lesion-guided pooling, so the multi-label pathology classifier reads localised evidence without being able to alter the segmentation it reads.}\label{fig:lesion-decoder}
