\caption{The shared ResNet-34 encoder. \textbf{(a)}~A strided $7{\times}7$ convolution and a
max-pool reduce resolution fourfold before four residual stages of 3, 4, 6 and 3 BasicBlocks;
each stage after the first halves the spatial extent and doubles the channel count, for a
cumulative downsampling factor of 32. The original classifier and global pooling are removed, so
the encoder emits a $16{\times}16{\times}512$ feature map rather than a class vector. Capacity is
concentrated at the coarsest scales: layer3 and layer4 together hold 19.94\,M of the encoder's
21.28\,M parameters. \textbf{(b)}~Structure of a BasicBlock. Within a stage the residual path is
an identity shortcut; in the first block of layer2, layer3 and layer4, where resolution and width
both change, it is replaced by a $1{\times}1$ strided convolution with batch
normalisation.}\label{fig:encoder}
