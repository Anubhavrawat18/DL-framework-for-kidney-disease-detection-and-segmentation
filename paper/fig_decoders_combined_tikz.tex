\caption{The two segmentation decoders in context. Both consume the \emph{same}
unpooled $512{\times}16{\times}16$ encoder feature map and are structurally
identical, differing only in their learned weights and supervision target. Each
emits a dense $512{\times}512$ logit map. The kidney logits are supervised by a
Dice term against the traced kidney mask. The lesion logits serve three
consumers: a Dice term against the rasterised lesion annotation, the anatomical
consistency term, and the lesion-guided pooling mechanism that supplies
localised evidence to the multi-label pathology classifier. Two wiring details
are easy to miss and are therefore drawn explicitly. First, the consistency term
pairs the predicted lesion map with the ground-truth kidney mask rather than
with the predicted kidney mask, so the constraint cannot be satisfied by
degrading kidney segmentation. Second, the path into lesion-guided pooling,
drawn in green, is detached, so the classification objective can read the lesion
map but cannot reshape it.}\label{fig:decoders-combined}
