\caption{Internal structure of a single segmentation decoder head.
\textbf{(a)}~Five successive up-blocks raise the encoder's
$512{\times}16{\times}16$ feature map back to full input resolution, halving the
channel count and doubling each spatial dimension at every stage, after which a
$1{\times}1$ convolution collapses the remaining sixteen channels to one logit
channel. The output is squeezed to $512{\times}512$ and carries raw logits; the
logistic function is applied by the loss and by the evaluation code rather than
inside the module. \textbf{(b)}~Each up-block is a $3{\times}3$ convolution with
padding one, batch normalisation, a ReLU activation and bilinear upsampling by a
factor of two. The design is deliberately plain: there are no skip connections
from the encoder, so the decoder must recover spatial detail from the
$16{\times}16$ bottleneck alone. The kidney and lesion decoders are structurally
identical instances of this head and hold 1,572,833 parameters each, differing
only in their learned weights and supervision target.}\label{fig:decoder}
